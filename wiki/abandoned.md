# Abandoned

*What was tried and dropped, and why. Nothing here comes back.*

Every entry back-links to the pages it affects, and those pages link back
here. That is what makes it fire: a rule one file away does not work, a rule
on the page you have open does.

Format:

```markdown
## Marit as narrator
tried: session 2 · dropped by: them
because: "she can't be surprised by her own story"
affects: [[characters/marit]] · [[threads/the-crossing]]
```

`dropped by:` is `them`, or the name of the voice that argued it down.
`patterns` counts those separately.
