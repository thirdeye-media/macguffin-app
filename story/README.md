# The story

`TREATMENT.md` is the deliverable. It exists from the first two minutes, complete and empty, so you can see the shape of what you are going to get before any of it is written.

Sections move `[empty]` → `[sketch]` → `[drawn]` → `[fixed]`.

**Only you may write `[fixed]`.** The apparatus never marks its own work finished.

`MACGUFFIN.md` is the object the story chases. `versions/` keeps every draft — nothing is ever lost, except what you explicitly dropped, which is gone from every version including the old ones.

**Edit these files directly.** That is the main way you work here. Change a paragraph, then say `ok` in the terminal. Your version wins over anything the apparatus wrote and over anything you said out loud earlier.

`_forms/` holds the five skeletons. You should not need to touch it.
