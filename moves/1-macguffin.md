# 1 · What the story chases

*The founding move. It converts one MacGuffin into another.*

Their folder is a MacGuffin in the loose sense — the thing that set this in motion. The story needs one in Hitchcock's sense: **the object everybody chases and nobody needs to understand.** This move makes the second out of the first.

## Write three candidates to file

Read `wiki/raw-notes.md`. Propose **three** objects the story could be built around. Write them to `story/MACGUFFIN.md`, not to the terminal.

Each candidate gets four lines and no more:

```markdown
## A · The renamed photograph
what it is:      a picture somebody retitled by hand
who wants it:    [open]
why it matters:  it is the only thing here that was touched twice
comes from:      raw-notes 4, 11
```

Rules for the three:

- **Each must point at a numbered line in `wiki/raw-notes.md`.** Write the numbers. A candidate you cannot source is one you invented.
- **Make them genuinely different.** Not one idea in three costumes. If two could be swapped without changing the story, you have two.
- **At least one should be something they would not have picked.** The dull object, the thing at the edge of the frame, the metadata rather than the image.
- A MacGuffin is a **thing**, not a theme. *A suitcase* is a MacGuffin. *Belonging* is not.

## The turn

```
→ story/MACGUFFIN.md   three candidates

pick one, or write your own into the file.
```

Then stop. **Do not recommend one.** If they ask which you prefer, say it in one sentence and move on.

## When they answer

If they wrote their own into the file, that one wins outright and you say nothing about the three.

Then, in this order:

1. Write `story/TREATMENT.md` §2 · The MacGuffin. Mark `[sketch]`.
2. Write §1 · Logline — one sentence, built from the object and nothing else yet. Mark `[sketch]`.
3. Make a wiki page for the object if it is a thing that recurs: `wiki/threads/<slug>.md`.
4. Log the two rejected candidates to `wiki/abandoned.md`, with `dropped by: them`.

```
→ story/TREATMENT.md  §1, §2  (2/7 sketched)
```

That is minute five, and there is a logline on disk. Then go to `moves/2-who-wants-it.md`.
