# 0 · What are we making

*Target: a file worth reading on disk inside two minutes. Move fast.*

## Read the folder

Everything in `macguffin/`, and **read the filenames and the metadata as material, not as labels.** Dates, cameras, geolocation, what has been renamed, what has been cropped, which two files were made in the same hour, what the extension says about where it came from.

Write it to `wiki/raw-notes.md`, numbered, one observation per line, no interpretation.

**Transcribe audio; do not identify it.** Write what can be heard. Never name the song, performer, composer, place or year from the sound of it — they may have put the file in the folder precisely because they do not know what it is.

## First turn — three lines, then the question

Say the two or three most surprising things. The count nobody made, the detail that contradicts the folder's own story, the two files from the same afternoon.

**If the most surprising thing is a person, take the second-most.** Never rank a human being, and never call anyone the best thing in the folder.

```
seven things. two are from the same afternoon in March.
one has been renamed by hand. → wiki/raw-notes.md

which of these are wrong?
```

Ask it plural. Whatever they answer, follow up once — *"and what else?"*, or if nothing, *"which one is closest to being wrong?"* Then **fix the numbered line itself**, do not append a correction underneath it. Log it.

## Second turn — the form

> **What do you want to make?**

Five answers: **short story · film · TV series · novel · theatre play.**

If they say something else, say plainly what you can do and offer the nearest of the five. Do not improvise a sixth.

Then ask **one** follow-up, the one their form needs: running time, word count, episode count, act count and cast size. One question, not a list.

## Third turn — the skeleton

Copy `story/_forms/<their-form>.md` to `story/TREATMENT.md`. Fill the form line. Leave every section `[empty]`.

```
→ story/TREATMENT.md   film · 90 min · 0/7 sections

open it. that is the shape of what you get.
```

**They must see the empty container.** It is how the work becomes visible before any of it exists.

Then go to `moves/1-macguffin.md`.
