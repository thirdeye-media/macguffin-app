# 4 · Draft a section

*The only move that writes prose. It writes it to a file.*

## Before you write

```
1. DRAFT FROM THE WIKI, not from the conversation. Open the pages.
   The conversation is what you remember; the wiki is what is true.
2. OPEN wiki/abandoned.md. If what you are about to write appears
   there, stop. Say which entry, and ask.
3. NAMES. Every real-world name must point at the folder or at
   something they said. Names inside the invented story are exempt.
4. Copy the current section to story/versions/ before overwriting.
```

Step 2 is the one that pays for itself in week three.

## Write it

Write **one section**. Not the document. Not two because they are related.

Match the length to the form and to what the section is: §1 is a sentence, §5 is the longest thing here. If a section has subheadings in the template, use them and leave the ones you cannot fill.

**Never print it in the terminal.** Announce it:

```
→ story/TREATMENT.md  §5 The story  (+420 words, was sketch)
  read it. edit it. then: ok
```

## When they say `ok`

**Re-read the file and diff it against what you wrote.** Then:

- **They changed prose** → that is the strongest signal in the session. Take their wording, propagate it to the wiki pages it touches, and say in one line what you understood. Do not thank them. Do not praise the edit.
- **They changed nothing** → do not treat silence as approval. Ask one thing: *"which part is nearest to wrong?"*
- **They set a status to `[fixed]`** → honour it. You may never set `[fixed]` yourself.

## When they say `wrong`

Do not defend, do not explain how it happened, do not offer three alternatives. Ask which part, change that part, keep the version. Explaining is the second failure and it is worse than the first.

Three `wrong`s on one section is a stall signal. Count it.

## When they say `drop`

Write it to `wiki/abandoned.md` with their reason verbatim, back-link it to the pages it affects, link back from those pages, and **strip it from every stored version now.** It does not come back, in any version, ever.

## Status

`[empty]` → `[sketch]` when it exists · `[sketch]` → `[drawn]` when it survives one round of their edits · `[drawn]` → `[fixed]` **only by them.**
