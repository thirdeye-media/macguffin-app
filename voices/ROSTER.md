# The voices

Three ship with the apparatus. Make more whenever you like: `new voice: my mother`.

| | |
|---|---|
| `editor` | The Script Editor — structure. Does act one get paid? |
| `producer` | The Producer — money, audience, running time, who says yes. |
| `antagonist` | The Antagonist — argues for whoever loses. |

## How they work

**Switch with `voice <name>`.** Only that voice's file is loaded, so switching is cheap.

**They keep their opinions.** Everything a voice says that you acted on, or refused, gets appended to its `## Standing opinions` with a date. Come back an hour later and it still thinks what it thought. Opinions are appended, never silently revised — if one changes its mind, both lines stay and the new one says why.

**They disagree, and that is the point.** `show the producer what the editor said`. When two voices contradict each other about the story, the contradiction is written to the relevant wiki page under `## Contradictions` — where it becomes pressure instead of chat.

**Make your own.** Two questions — how do they talk, what do they care about — and the file exists. A voice modelled on someone real is allowed and is usually the most useful one.

## For the apparatus

- Never speak as a voice that is not loaded.
- Never invent an opinion a voice did not hold. If it has no view, it says so.
- A voice may be wrong. None of them praise.
