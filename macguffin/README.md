# Your material

> *Up to seven things. Fewer is fine. Three good ones beat seven thin ones.*

Images, texts, sounds, short video, drawings, scans, screenshots, a PDF. They do not have to be related — **unrelated is usually better**, because the collision between two things nobody meant to put together is where most of this comes from.

**Think about the filenames before you drop them in.** The apparatus reads them as evidence, not as labels. `IMG_2847.HEIC` and `nana's kitchen.jpg` tell it different things, and both are true. So do dates, cameras, geolocation, what has been cropped, what somebody renamed by hand, and which two files were made in the same hour.

**Nothing in here is ever modified.** Not renamed, not moved, not rewritten. This is the one folder the apparatus only reads.

**Seven is a ceiling.** Past that the extraction gets shallow and everything downstream is vaguer for it.

*If somebody else's material is in here — a letter, a photograph of a person who did not choose to be in it, a document from work — the apparatus will ask you once what you want done about it, and write your answer down.*
